Kelavani Mandal, established in 1994, is a non-profit premier trust of India that works with the aim to provide social service through education. The organization has a social lineage of more than 125 years.

Kelavani Mandal has been established with the distinct objective of creating state-of-the-art educational facilities. The trust is a democratically managed institution known for its transparent functioning and integrity.

Vision

To be a leading institution that provides innovative, accessible, and quality online education, empowering learners to achieve academic excellence and contribute to the global knowledge economy.

Mission

  • To deliver learner-centric online degree programs that meet the highest standards of academic rigor and relevance.
  • To foster a culture of continuous improvement, ensuring the online education experience is enriching, engaging, and aligned with industry needs.
  • To leverage technology and research to enhance teaching, learning, and assessment processes, making education accessible to a diverse and global student population.
  • To equip students with the skills, knowledge, and values necessary to thrive in a rapidly changing world.
