# Online BBA

## Course overview

The Online BBA programme is meticulously designed to empower learners with knowledge, skills, and competencies necessary for achieving success in the ever-evolving field of business administration. This programme is thoughtfully structured to facilitate the development of specialized expertise within the distinct business areas such as Finance Management, Human Resource Management and Marketing Management.

Duration:

3 years

Total credits:

133

Annual Fee:

50,000 INR

## Eligibility Criteria

Students who have successfully completed the secondary examination (10+2) pattern or equivalent are eligible to apply for admission. The specific minimum percentage of marks or Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) required for eligibility will be determined by the Admission committee. Applicant should demonstrate a sufficient level of knowledge and understanding of the English language.
