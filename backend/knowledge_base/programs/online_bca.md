# Online BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)

## Course Overview

The online BCA programme is aligned with CMPICA's mission to create a learning environment that fosters creativity, innovation, and critical thinking among students. The programme is designed to provide a world-class education in computer science and applications, with a focus on developing skilled professionals who can make valuable contributions to the industry and society.

The programme provides opportunities for students to engage in practical and project-based learning, which helps them develop teamwork skills, leadership skills, and communication skills.

- **Duration**: 3 years (6 semesters)
- **Total Credits**: 145
- **Annual Fee**: ₹50,000 INR

## Eligibility Criteria

Candidate should have passed 12th standard (or equivalent) any stream with English as a subject and according to the regulations for admission decided by CHARUSAT from time to time.

## Programme Outcomes

At the end of the programme, the student should be able to:

- Analyze, evaluate and formulate workable computing solutions to real world problems using computing methods.
- Apply proficiencies of communication, teamwork and management skills with spirit of entrepreneurship.

What will you be able to do?

- Understand the requirement of computing problem and implement an effective solution.
- Successfully take up various available career options in the relevant domain.

## FAQs

### About the BCA Programme

**Q: What is the aim of the BCA Programme at CHARUSAT?**
A: The BCA Programme aims to provide a sound knowledge of computer applications and equip the learner with the necessary skills to excel in the IT industry. The contemporary curriculum of BCA is designed to provide necessary software skills and industry exposure to students through project-driven courses and hands-on experience.

**Q: What is the eligibility requirement for the Online BCA programme?**
A: 10+2 in any stream is required, equivalent to all eligible boards (including NIOS Board).

**Q: What documents are required for BCA admission?**
A: The following mandatory documents are required:
- Proof of identity (any one): Aadhaar card, passport, voter's ID, PAN card with photograph, or any other Govt.-issued photo identity card
- Proof of address (any one): Aadhaar card, passport, voter's ID, gas bill, bank statement, or Electricity Bill
- Class 10 marksheet
- Class 12 marksheet

**Q: Is the BCA programme fully online? Do I need to attend physical classes?**
A: The online programmes at CHARUSAT are created in accordance with UGC regulations. CHARUSAT uses technology-assisted procedures and resources to provide comprehensive programmes, flexible learning opportunities, and e-learning content. There is no requirement for students to physically attend sessions. Students can study online from any location as long as they have a phone, laptop, tablet, or computer connected to the internet.

**Q: How does the admission process work for the BCA programme?**
A: The admissions staff of CHARUSAT thoroughly reviews all submitted applications for the online BCA degree to determine eligibility. In case the admission council requires any clarification or more details, the student will be contacted via email, SMS, or phone call.

**Q: Is WES evaluation available for CHARUSAT's BCA degree?**
A: Yes. At CHARUSAT, obtaining a WES evaluation is a streamlined process that can be completed from the comfort of your own home via the website portal. The WES process at CHARUSAT is conducted online through email service, which ensures a prompt response time.

### About CHARUSAT University

**Q: Is CHARUSAT accredited?**

### About Examinations

**Q: How are exams conducted for the Online BCA programme?**
A: All exams are conducted in online mode. Students are under observation (proctored) during their examinations.

**Q: What types of questions are asked in the BCA exams?**
A: Both MCQ (Multiple Choice Questions) and Descriptive type questions are included in the exams.

**Q: Can I skip a question and come back to it during the exam?**
A: Yes, you can skip any question and come back to the same question later during the exam.

**Q: How are exam dates assigned?**
A: The university will send you date sheets and you can select an appropriate date from that sheet.
