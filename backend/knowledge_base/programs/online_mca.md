# Online MCA (Master of Computer Applications)

## Course Overview

The online Master of Computer Applications (MCA) programme is designed to provide advanced learning opportunities to a diverse group of non-traditional learners, including full-time working professionals, entrepreneurs, individuals residing in remote regions, non-residents, and homemakers. This program is ideal for candidates who face challenges in pursuing education in a traditional classroom setting due to time, cost, or distance constraints.

The online MCA program offers a flexible and accessible platform for learners to enhance their technical knowledge and skills in computer applications, covering areas such as software development, database management, networking, and cybersecurity. This program equips students with the expertise needed for successful careers in the rapidly evolving tech industry.

- **Duration**: 2 years (4 semesters)
- **Total Credits**: 108
- **Annual Fee**: ₹75,000 INR

## Eligibility Criteria

Candidate should have passed Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) any stream with English as a subject is eligible for admission to the MCA Programme and according to the regulations for admission decided by regulatory authorities and CHARUSAT from time to time.

## FAQs

### About the MCA Programme

**Q: What is the aim of the MCA Programme at CHARUSAT?**
A: The MCA Programme aims to provide a sound knowledge of computer applications and equip the learner with the necessary skills to excel in the IT industry. The contemporary curriculum of MCA is designed to provide necessary software skills and industry exposure to students through project-driven courses and hands-on experience.

**Q: What is the eligibility requirement for the Online MCA programme?**
A: Candidates must have a Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in any stream with English as a subject.

**Q: What documents are required for MCA admission?**
A: The following mandatory documents are required:
- Proof of identity (any one): Aadhaar card, passport, voter's ID, PAN card with photograph, or any other Govt.-issued photo identity card
- Proof of address (any one): Aadhaar card, passport, voter's ID, gas bill, bank statement, or Electricity Bill
- Class 10 marksheet
- Class 12 marksheet
- Bachelor's degree certificate and marksheets

**Q: Is the MCA programme fully online? Do I need to attend physical classes?**
A: The online programmes at CHARUSAT are created in accordance with UGC regulations. CHARUSAT uses technology-assisted procedures and resources to provide comprehensive programmes, flexible learning opportunities, and e-learning content. There is no requirement for students to physically attend sessions. Students can study online from any location as long as they have a phone, laptop, tablet, or computer connected to the internet.

**Q: How does the admission process work for the MCA programme?**
A: The admissions staff of CHARUSAT thoroughly reviews all submitted applications for the online MCA degree to determine eligibility. In case the admission council requires any clarification or more details, the student will be contacted via email, SMS, or phone call.

**Q: Is WES evaluation available for CHARUSAT's MCA degree?**
A: Yes. At CHARUSAT, obtaining a WES evaluation is a streamlined process that can be completed from the comfort of your own home via the website portal. The WES process at CHARUSAT is conducted online through email service, which ensures a prompt response time.

### About Examinations

**Q: How are exams conducted for the Online MCA programme?**
A: All exams are conducted in online mode. Students are under observation (proctored) during their examinations.

**Q: What types of questions are asked in the MCA exams?**
A: Both MCQ (Multiple Choice Questions) and Descriptive type questions are included in the exams.

**Q: Can I skip a question and come back to it during the exam?**
A: Yes, you can skip any question and come back to the same question later during the exam.

**Q: How are exam dates assigned?**
A: The university will send you date sheets and you can select an appropriate date from that sheet.
