# CHARUSAT Online Examination – Complete Guide

## Overview
CHARUSAT (Charotar University of Science and Technology) conducts online examinations using the **LM Exam Suite** software by **Littlemore Innovation Labs**. The examination is **Internet-based and remote proctored**, meaning students take exams from home using their own laptop/desktop with webcam and microphone monitoring throughout the examination.

---

## Step-by-Step: How to Take an Online Exam

### Step 1: Download the LM Exam Software
- Visit [https://charusat.lmexamsuite.com/downloads](https://charusat.lmexamsuite.com/downloads)
- Download the LM Exam Suite application for your operating system (Windows/macOS/Linux)
- Save the installer to your computer

### Step 2: Install the Software
- Run the downloaded installer file
- Follow the on-screen installation prompts
- Complete the installation process

### Step 3: Open the LM Exam Software
- Launch the LM Exam Suite application from your desktop or start menu
- You will see the login screen with fields for Student ID and Password

### Step 4: Enter Your Login Credentials
- **Student ID / Roll No.:** Enter your university-provided student ID (e.g., 2024-001)
- **Password:** Enter your assigned password
- Click the **"Login"** button

### Step 5: Validate Your Profile
- After login, you will see the profile validation screen
- Verify your details:
  - **Name** (e.g., Student01)
  - **Student ID / Roll No.**
  - **Institution:** Charotar University of Science and Technology
  - **Mobile Number**
- Check the **"I Agree to Terms & Conditions"** checkbox

### Step 6: Click "Proceed"
- After verifying your profile and agreeing to terms, click the **"Proceed"** button

### Step 7: Device Registration (One-Time Only)
- A popup will appear: **"Device Registration – User not registered on this device. Do you want to register?"**
- Click **"Register"** to register your device
- This is a **one-time process** – you won't be asked again on the same device

### Step 8: Dashboard
- After successful login and registration, you will see the **Dashboard** with:
  - Your **Roll No.**, **Program**, **Mobile No.**, and **Age**
  - **"Start Practice Exam"** button (for mock/practice exams)
  - **"My Calendar"** showing exam dates
  - **Exam History** table with columns: Code, Program, Subject, Date & Time, Attendance, Upload Status
- The dashboard shows your scheduled exams and past exam records

### Step 9: Read Terms and Conditions
- Read all the **Terms and Conditions** carefully before starting the exam
- The LM Exam Suite Terms of Use govern your use of the examination software

### Step 10: Settings (Optional)
- Click the settings icon to access options:
  - **Theme Mode:** Switch between Light/Dark mode
  - **Right-To-Left Mode:** For left-handed users
  - **Reset** button to restore defaults

### Step 11: Dashboard Tutorial
- A guided tutorial explains key elements of the application:
  - **Page title** (Dashboard)
  - **Dark/Light Mode** toggle
  - **Storage Indicator** (e.g., 4.13 GB)
  - **Battery Indicator**
  - **Logout** button
- These elements appear in the **Application Header Bar**

### Step 12: Start Your Exam
- From the Dashboard, click on your **exam schedule** (your subject name or code)
- If available, you may also use the **"Start Practice Exam"** option for mock tests

### Step 13: Microphone Detection
- The system will test your **microphone**
- You will see the **"Mic Detection"** screen
- Speak a small sentence so the system can detect your mic (e.g., say "Hi, My Microphone is Active!")
- Once detected, the message **"We can hear you"** will appear
- Click **"Proceed"** to continue

### Step 14: Face Detection (Unlock Exam)
- The system will activate your **webcam** for face detection
- A popup shows **"Do's and Dont's"** for **Unlock - Face Detection**:
  - Ensure the face is clearly visible on the camera
  - Sit upright in front of the camera to enable face detection
  - Ensure there is good lighting on the face
  - A **green square** will be marked around the face once detected
  - The **Unlock** button will be enabled after face detection
- Once the green square appears around your face, click **"Bring it on!!"** to start the exam

### Step 15: Select Input Mode & Start Exam
- Select the **"Keyboard Mode"** option and click **"Confirm"**
- The exam paper loads with:
  - **Paper Name** (e.g., Practice Paper)
  - **Total Marks** (e.g., 32)
  - **Duration** (e.g., 3:00:00 hrs)
  - **Time Left** countdown timer
  - **Bio-Break** pause button
  - **Section tabs** (e.g., S-A, Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Q5)
  - **Section Time Left** indicator
- Questions appear with multiple-choice options
- Use **"Mark for later"** flag to bookmark questions for review

### Step 16: Exam Tutorial (In-Exam)
- An in-exam tutorial highlights:
  - **Exam Name and other Details** (e.g., [PQM103] Product Quality Management 3)
  - **Marks** and **Duration** information
  - **Time Left** indicator
  - **Video Panel** for monitoring
  - **Bio-Break Button**

### Step 17: Navigate Sections
- The exam may have multiple sections (Section A, Section B, Section C, etc.)
- Navigate between questions within a section using the question tabs
- Some questions may include images or diagrams

### Step 18: Home/Exam Details View
- Click the **Home icon** to see an overview:
  - **Roll No.** and **Name**
  - **Exam Details** table with columns: Section, Flagged, Pending, Attempted
  - **"Review Answer Sheet"** button
  - **"Finish Section"** button for each section
  - **"Finish Exam →"** button

### Step 19: Finish a Section
- When done with a section, click **"Finish Section - A"** (or the relevant section)
- A confirmation popup appears: **"Finish Section Confirmation – Do you want to finish the current section and move to next? You will not be able to return to this section later. Balance time of this section will be added to next section."**
- Click **"Yes"** to confirm, or **"No"** to continue working
- **Important:** Once you finish a section, you **cannot go back** to it

### Step 20: Complete Remaining Sections
- Repeat the same process for subsequent sections (Section B, Section C, etc.)
- The same "Finish Section Confirmation" dialog will appear each time

### Step 21: Finish Exam
- After completing all sections (or when the last section is done), click **"Finish Exam →"**
- A confirmation popup appears: **"Exam End Confirmation – Do you really want to finish the exam? You will not be able to make any changes after exam is finished?"**
- Click **"Yes"** to submit your exam, or **"No"** to continue

### Step 22: Submission Confirmation
- After clicking "Yes", wait for the system to process
- When you see the message: **"✓ Answersheet has been submitted."**
- Click **"Go to dashboard"** to return to the main screen
- You can now exit the software

## Technical Specifications for Internet-Based Remote Proctored Exams

### Minimum System Requirements

| Hardware/Software | Minimum Requirements |
|---|---|
| **Operating System** | Windows 10 (Pro, Enterprise, and Education editions) OR Windows 8/8.1 (32-bit and 64-bit, all editions) OR Windows 7 (32-bit and 64-bit, all editions) OR macOS OR Linux |
| **Processor** | 2 core (4 vCPU) or above with 2.2 GHz or more clock speed |
| **RAM** | 2 GB or above |
| **Web Camera** | Webcam must have a minimum resolution of 640x480 @ 15 fps. Webcam may be internal or external and connected to the computer. The camera must run natively in the OS and not with help of other software. |
| **Microphone** | Built-in microphones are acceptable, as are external microphones |
| **Screen Resolution** | 1024 x 768 and above |
| **Internet** | 2 MBPS or more (Upload Speed) – dedicated and continuous connectivity. (Preferably Wired) |

**Note:** Up-to-date Service pack, device drivers, and security patches in test taking system are required.

## Technical Guidelines for Exam Participation

- **Security Software:** Certain security programs, such as antivirus or Internet security software, may interfere with the exam delivery. These may need to be temporarily disabled.
- **Administrator Rights:** Ensure that the Windows/system login being used has administrator rights.
- **Virtual Desktop:** Virtual desktops must be disabled before the exam.
- **Extended Monitors:** Do not use extended monitors during the exam.
- **Audio and Microphone:** Verify that both the audio and microphone settings are not on mute.

---

## System Requirements and Best Practices

- Ensure an uninterrupted power supply, stable Internet connectivity with the required bandwidth, and systems configured as per the examination requirements.
- The exam can only be conducted if both the webcam and microphone are enabled on your desktop or laptop.
- Close all applications that use the camera, microphone, or screen-sharing features prior to the start of the exam.
- Disable any third-party settings or software to prevent disruptions during the test.
- The Graphical Processing Unit (GPU) should be from 2017 or newer.
- Avoid switching networks during the exam, as it may cause interruptions. In such cases, you may need to log back into the system after a 3-minute cooldown period.
- If a "face not detected" error occurs during photo capture, please attempt again in a well-lit environment where the light is on your face and not coming from behind.

## Guidelines for Remote Proctored Examinations

The remote proctored examination system monitors your test using a webcam, microphone, and screen sharing. Adherence to the following guidelines is mandatory:

- **Device Requirements:** Candidates must use a desktop or laptop equipped with a high-quality webcam and microphone, with stable internet connectivity (minimum 2 Mbps upload speed).
- **Webcam Placement:** The webcam should be positioned directly in front of you, preferably mounted on top of the monitor, to clearly display your face throughout the examination. Only built-in or monitor-mounted cameras are permitted; alternative setups are not allowed and may lead to disciplinary action.
- **Webcam and Microphone Activation:** Both the webcam and microphone must remain active for the entire exam duration. Any disruption in their functionality may be interpreted as an attempt at malpractice.
- **Camera Visibility:** Your face must remain fully visible within the camera frame at all times. Leaving the camera's field of view is strictly prohibited.
- **Lighting Requirements:** Ensure that your face is well-lit, with no strong light sources behind you.
- **No Obstructions:** Hair, clothing, hands, or any other objects must not obscure your face at any point during the exam.
- **Eyewear Restrictions:** Sunglasses are not permitted. Only transparent, non-reflective glasses are allowed.
- **Room Conditions:** You must take the exam alone, with no other individuals present in the room.
- **Movement Restrictions:** Leaving your workstation during the examination is forbidden. Violating this rule may result in termination of the exam or other disciplinary actions.
- **Noise Control:** Ensure a quiet environment throughout the exam.
- **Browser Settings:** The exam must be taken in a browser set to full-screen mode. Switching to other applications or browser tabs is not permitted, except for accessing scanned documents for upload.
- **Application Restrictions:** Close all programs or applications that use the webcam, microphone, or screen sharing, such as Skype, Zoom, or MS Teams, during the examination.
- **Prohibition on Recording:** Recording any part of the examination is strictly forbidden.
- **Unfair Practices:** Any attempt to use unfair means during the examination will result in disqualification.
- **Mobile Phone Usage:** Mobile phones may only be used to scan handwritten answer scripts and transfer scanned files to the test-taking system.
- **Calculator Usage:** A calculator is provided within the examination tool. For numerical subjects, you may use a scientific calculator.
- **Submission Deadline:** The exam duration includes time for scanning and uploading your answers. Ensure all answers are uploaded within the allotted 2-hour exam duration. Responses sent via email or other methods will not be evaluated.
- **Re-Login Process:** If you are logged out during the examination for any reason, your previous answers will be saved automatically. You may re-login after a 3-minute interval.
- **Technical Assistance:** For any technical issues during the exam, contact the exam helpline provided in the email.

## Acts Considered Malpractice or Unfair Means

The following acts are considered malpractice and may lead to **disqualification** or **disciplinary action**:

- **Unauthorized Materials:** Using additional screens, devices, or unauthorized materials to copy answers.
- **Prohibited Software:** Accessing IDEs, online programming environments, language models like ChatGPT, or any unauthorized desktop applications (except for accessing scanned responses).
- **Communication During Exam:** Answering mobile calls or conversing with others during the exam.
- **Leaving the Screen:** Leaving the examination or screen without prior notice via chat.
- **Disabling Equipment:** Covering your face, disabling the camera, or disabling the microphone.
- **Presence of Others:** Having another person present in the examination room.
- **Non-Compliance:** Failing to adhere to the examination guidelines or the instructions provided by the proctor.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What software is used for online exams at CHARUSAT?**
A: CHARUSAT uses the **LM Exam Suite** by Littlemore Innovation Labs for conducting online proctored exams.

**Q: Where can I download the exam software?**
A: Download from [https://charusat.lmexamsuite.com/downloads](https://charusat.lmexamsuite.com/downloads)

**Q: Do I need a webcam and microphone for the exam?**
A: Yes, both webcam (minimum 640x480 @ 15fps) and microphone are mandatory. The exam cannot start without them.

**Q: What internet speed is required?**
A: Minimum 2 MBPS upload speed with dedicated, continuous connectivity. Wired internet is preferred.

**Q: Can I use my mobile phone during the exam?**
A: Mobile phones may only be used to scan handwritten answer scripts. All other mobile use is prohibited.

**Q: What happens if I get disconnected during the exam?**
A: Your answers are saved automatically. You can re-login after a 3-minute cooldown period.

**Q: Can I go back to a previous section after finishing it?**
A: No. Once you click "Finish Section" and confirm, you cannot return to that section. The remaining time from that section is added to the next section.

**Q: What if my face is not detected?**
A: Ensure you are in a well-lit environment with light on your face (not from behind). Try again after adjusting your position and lighting.

**Q: Is there a practice exam available?**
A: Yes, you can use the "Start Practice Exam" button on the dashboard for mock tests.

**Q: What happens if I use unfair means?**
A: Any attempt at malpractice will result in disqualification and possible disciplinary action.

**Q: Do I need to register my device?**
A: Yes, the first time you use a device, the software will ask for a one-time device registration.

**Q: What operating systems are supported?**
A: Windows 10/8.1/8/7, macOS, and Linux are supported.
