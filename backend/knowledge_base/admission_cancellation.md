# Admission Cancellation

Cancellation form: https://charusat.online/catalogs/Adm_Can_Frm.docx  
Form header: CHAROTAR UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (Accredited Grade 'A+' by NAAC), CHARUSAT Campus, Off Nadiad-Petlad Highway, Changa-388421 (Gujarat) INDIA, Ph: 91-2697-265008, 265011

## How to cancel

1. Apply using the **prescribed form** ([Adm_Can_Frm.docx](https://charusat.online/catalogs/Adm_Can_Frm.docx)).
2. The form must be **duly signed by the student** and **countersigned by the parent/guardian**.
3. Send a **scanned copy of the application** to the **programme coordinator**.
4. Enclose the required original documents with the form (see below).

## Fields the form asks for (from the form table)

- Program Name
- Current Semester
- Student ID
- Admission Quota (General / NRI Quota)
- 1) Name of the Student (as per Enrolment / Registration)
- 2) Address for Communication (with PIN Code), Phone (R)/(M), E-mail
- 3) Gender (Male / Female)
- 4) Reason for cancellation of Admission (Admitted elsewhere or planning for it / No interest in the program / Economical constraint / Social reason). Also a brief reason for cancellation.
- 5) Details for refund to be made by NEFT (CHARUSAT will issue the cheque for refund in the name of the candidate; details below are requested): Name of Beneficiary Account, Bank Name and Branch, Account No., IFSC code of the Bank

Declarations/signatures requested on the form:
- Declaration by student (place/date/signature)
- Institute use: Student Counsellor’s Remarks (Name & Signature), Program Coordinator’s Remarks (Name & Signature), CCOE Remarks (Name & Signature)
- University use: verified by various officials including Account Officer (Tuition Fees Refund / Deposit Refund / Admission Cancel with dates)

## Documents to be enclosed (original)

From the form: **1. Fee receipts** and **2. Admission Slip/Allotment Letter**.
