# CHARUSAT Online Admissions Guide

---

## General Eligibility Requirements

### For Online BCA
- **Medium of Instruction:** English should be a subject in Class 12
- **Age:** Minimum 18 years old at time of admission

### For Online MCA
- **Minimum Qualification:** Bachelor's degree in any stream
- **Medium of Instruction:** English must be a subject or medium of instruction in undergraduate program
- **Age:** No specific age limit

### For Online BBA
- **Minimum Qualification:** 12th pass in any stream
- **Medium of Instruction:** English should be a subject in Class 12

### For Online MBA
- **Minimum Qualification:** Bachelor's degree in any stream
- **Work Experience:** Preferred (not mandatory)

## Online Admission Process

### Step-by-Step Application

**Step 1: Portal Registration**
- Visit: https://admission.charusat.ac.in/
- Click "New Registration" or "Apply Now"

**Step 2: Program Selection**
- Select the desired program:
  - BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)
  - MCA (Master of Computer Applications)
  - BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)
  - MBA (Master of Business Administration)
- Online/Distance mode

**Step 3: Personal Information**
- Full name (as per Class 10 certificate)
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Contact number
- Email address
- Residential address

**Step 4: Academic Background**
- 10th board and percentage
- 12th board and percentage
- Bachelor's degree details (for postgraduate programs)
- Diploma details (if applicable)

**Step 5: Document Upload**
- Upload signature
- Upload Class 10 marksheet
- Upload Class 12 marksheet
- Upload Bachelor's degree certificate (if applicable)
- Upload identity proof
- Upload address proof
- Upload any additional certificates

**Step 6: Application Fee Payment**
- Payment methods accepted:
  - Credit Card
  - Debit Card
  - Net Banking
  - UPI
- Keep payment receipt for reference

**Step 7: Form Review & Submission**
- Review all entered information
- Verify document uploads
- Accept Terms & Conditions
- Submit application

**Step 8: Verification & Processing**
- Admissions team reviews your application
- Documents are verified
- Eligibility criteria are checked
- You may be contacted for clarifications via email, SMS, or phone

**Step 9: Admission Decision**
- Receive admission status via email
- Provisional admission letter issued
- Instructions for fee payment

**Step 10: Enrollment & Fee Payment**
- Complete enrollment formalities
- Pay first-semester/annual fees
- Receive LMS login credentials
- Receive Student ID

## Refund Policy

### Admission Cancellation & Refund

#### Refund Slabs

**Slab 1: Early Cancellation**
- **Timing:** Request received before admission closure date OR before commencement of classes (whichever is later)
- **Refund:** Full fee minus Rs. 1,500/- as administrative charges

**Slab 2: Mid-Session Cancellation**
- **Timing:** Request received after commencement of academic session
- **Refund:** Pro-rata deduction from commencement date of classes, minus Rs. 1,500/- as processing charges

**Slab 3: Late Cancellation**
- **Timing:** Request received after first mid-term review date
- **Refund:** No refund except for security deposit only

### How to Request Cancellation

**Cancellation Form:**
- Download: https://charusat.online/catalogs/Adm_Can_Frm.docx
- Fill completely
- Submit to university office

**Processing:**
- Processing time: 10-15 working days
- Refund mode: Cheque or NEFT
- Refunds processed by Accounts Section of CHARUSAT

### Important Notes

1. All cancellation applications processed after academic session starts
2. Student liable to pay current semester fees if cancellation requested after deadline
