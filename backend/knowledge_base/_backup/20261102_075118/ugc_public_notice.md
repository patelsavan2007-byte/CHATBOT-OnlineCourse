# UGC Guidelines for Online Education Programs

## Important Information for Students

### About UGC Regulations
- The University Grants Commission (UGC) sets standards for online and distance education in India
- CHARUSAT follows UGC Regulations 2020 (with 2021 and 2022 amendments)
- These ensure quality education in online programs

### Before You Enroll - Check These

#### 1. Verify University Recognition
- **Always check**: Is CHARUSAT recognized for online programs in your chosen year?
- **Website**: Visit [UGC DEB website](https://deb.ugc.ac.in/)
- **What to look for**: 
  - Year-wise recognition status
  - List of approved programs
  - Any restrictions or conditions

#### 2. Check Program Approval
- Each online program must be specifically approved by UGC
- Verify your chosen program (BCA, BBA, MBA, MCA) is on the approved list
- Approval status can change year-to-year

#### 3. Review University Website
- CHARUSAT must publish:
  - UGC application details
  - Affidavits and approvals
  - Program-specific information
- Read all notices before admission

### What You Must Know

#### Important Dates
- **Admission deadlines**: Vary by academic session
- **Current deadlines**: Check CHARUSAT website for latest dates
- **Never miss**: Final admission dates published by UGC

#### Prohibited Arrangements
- **No franchising**: CHARUSAT cannot franchise online programs
- **Territorial jurisdiction**: Programs must operate within approved regions
- **Direct delivery**: All teaching must come directly from CHARUSAT

### Your Responsibilities as a Student

#### Before Admission
1. Verify CHARUSAT's current recognition status
2. Confirm your program is approved
3. Check admission deadlines
4. Read all published notices

#### During Program
1. Follow UGC guidelines
2. Report any concerns about program delivery
3. Keep records of all communications

#### After Completion
1. Ensure degree meets UGC standards
2. Verify degree recognition if needed for further studies
3. Keep all academic records safe

### Common Questions

#### Q: Is CHARUSAT recognized for online programs?
**A**: Yes, but check for the specific year and program on UGC website.

#### Q: What if I enroll in an unapproved program?
**A**: Your degree may not be recognized. Always verify first.

#### Q: How often should I check recognition status?
**A**: Before each academic year's admission.

#### Q: What if there are changes after I enroll?
**A**: CHARUSAT must inform students of any status changes.

### Important Updates
