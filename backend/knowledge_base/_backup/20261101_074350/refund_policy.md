# CHARUSAT Online Programs - Refund Policy

## Simple Refund Rules

This policy explains when and how you can get refunds for CHARUSAT online programs.

### Quick Summary
| When You Cancel | Refund Amount | Processing Fee |
|----------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Before classes start** | Almost full refund | ₹1,500 deducted |
| **After classes start** | Partial (pro-rata) refund | ₹1,500 deducted |
| **After first mid-term** | No refund | Security deposit only |

## Detailed Refund Rules

### 1. Early Cancellation (Best Refund)
**When**: Cancel **before** admission closes OR before classes start (whichever is later)

**What you get**:
- Full fee minus ₹1,500 processing charge
- Example: Paid ₹50,000 → Get ₹48,500 back

**How to cancel**:
- Submit cancellation form to University Office
- Use form: https://charusat.online/catalogs/Adm_Can_Frm.docx
- Get confirmation receipt

### 2. Mid-Session Cancellation (Partial Refund)
**When**: Cancel **after** classes start but **before** first mid-term review

**What you get**:
- Pro-rata calculation from class start date
- Minus ₹1,500 processing charge
- Example: Used 1 month of 6-month course → Get 5/6 of fee back

**How it's calculated**:
```
Refund = (Remaining days ÷ Total days) × Fee - ₹1,500
```

### 3. Late Cancellation (No Refund)
**When**: Cancel **after** first mid-term review

**What you get**:
- **No tuition fee refund**
- Security deposit returned (if applicable)
- Must pay current semester fees

## Important Details

### What You Need to Know

#### 1. Processing Time
- Refunds processed after academic session starts
- Allow 4-6 weeks for processing
- Keep all payment receipts

#### 2. Payment Methods
Refunds come via:
- **Cheque**: Mailed to your address
- **NEFT**: Bank transfer (preferred)
- Provide correct bank details

#### 3. Security Deposit
- Separate from tuition fees
- Returned in all cases (unless forfeited for damages)
- Different refund timeline

### Step-by-Step Refund Process

#### Step 1: Submit Cancellation
1. Download cancellation form
2. Fill completely and accurately
3. Submit to University Office
4. Get acknowledgment receipt

#### Step 2: Wait for Processing
1. University reviews your request
2. Calculates refund amount
3. Approves or requests more information
4. Sends confirmation

#### Step 3: Receive Payment
1. Choose refund method
2. Provide correct details
3. Wait for processing
4. Confirm receipt

### Common Scenarios

#### Scenario A: Change of Mind
- Realized program isn't right for you
- Cancelled before classes
- **Result**: Full refund minus ₹1,500

#### Scenario B: Personal Emergency
- Can't continue due to health/family issues
- Cancelled after 2 months of 6-month course
- **Result**: 4 months refund (pro-rata) minus ₹1,500

#### Scenario C: Academic Reasons
- Failing or struggling with course
- Cancelled after mid-term
- **Result**: No refund, pay current fees

### Important Dates to Remember

#### Key Deadlines
1. **Admission closure date**: Check program details
2. **Class start date**: Provided in schedule
3. **First mid-term review**: Usually 6-8 weeks into course

#### Don't Miss
- Submit cancellation before deadlines
- Keep copies of all documents
- Follow up if no response

### Special Cases

#### Technical Issues
- Platform not working properly
- Contact tech support first
- Refund only if issue can't be resolved

#### Program Changes
- CHARUSAT changes course significantly
- You're not satisfied with changes
- May qualify for special refund

#### Medical Emergencies
- Provide medical certificates
- Special consideration possible
- Contact admissions office directly

### What's Not Refundable

#### Always Non-Refundable
1. **Processing fee** (₹1,500 in all cases)
2. **Used portion** of course (after classes start)
3. **Administrative charges** (if applicable)

#### Sometimes Refundable
1. **Security deposit** (usually refundable)
2. **Lab fees** (if not used)
3. **Material fees** (if materials not sent)
