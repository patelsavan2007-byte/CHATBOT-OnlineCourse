# Online MBA (Master of Business Administration) - Complete Guide

## Overview

The Online Master of Business Administration (MBA) programme at CHARUSAT is designed to provide advanced learning opportunities to working professionals, entrepreneurs, remote learners, and non-traditional students who cannot pursue traditional education due to time, cost, or distance constraints.

### Program Details

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Duration** | 2 years (4 semesters) |
| **Total Credits** | 100 |
| **Annual Fee** | ₹75,000 INR |
| **Intake (2024-25)** | 1,000 students |
| **Mode** | 100% Online |
| **Delivery** | Asynchronous with synchronous sessions |
| **Specializations** | Finance, HR, Marketing |
| **Medium of Instruction** | English |

## Admission Process

### Step-by-Step Application Guide

**Step 1: Online Registration**
- Visit: https://admission.charusat.ac.in/
- Click "New Registration" or "Apply Now"

**Step 2: Program Selection**
- Select "MBA – Online" from program list
- Choose preferred specialization (Finance/HR/Marketing)
- Select academic year and intake

**Step 3: Personal Information**
- Complete personal details (name, DOB, gender)
- Contact information (phone, email, address)
- Work experience details (if applicable)

**Step 4: Academic Background**
- Class 10 and 12 details
- Bachelor's degree details (university, degree, marks)
- Any additional qualifications
- Professional certifications

**Step 5: Document Upload**
- Upload signature
- Upload all educational certificates
- Upload identity and address proof
- Upload work experience certificates (if any)
- Upload any additional supporting documents

**Step 6: Application Fee Payment**
- Pay non-refundable application fee
- Online payment gateway available
- Keep payment receipt for reference
- Fee amount: Check admission portal for current

**Step 7: Form Review & Submission**
- Review all entered information carefully
- Accept Terms & Conditions
- Submit final application

**Step 8: Admission Review Process**
- Document verification and eligibility check
- Background verification if required
- You may be contacted for clarifications or interviews

**Step 9: Admission Decision**
- Receive admission decision via email
- Provisional admission letter issued
- Instructions for enrollment and fee payment
- Timeline: Usually within 2-3 weeks of application

**Step 10: Enrollment & Orientation**
- Complete enrollment formalities
- Pay first-semester fees
- Receive LMS login credentials
- Attend online orientation program
- Access course materials and schedule

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### About MBA Programme

**Q: Is the Online MBA equivalent to a regular MBA?**

A: Yes, the Online MBA from CHARUSAT is UGC-recognized and equivalent to a regular MBA. The degree certificate does not mention "online" mode.

### Academic Questions

**Q: How are exams conducted?**

A: Online proctored exams using secure software with webcam monitoring, microphone detection, and strict anti-cheating measures.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What technical requirements are needed?**

A: Computer/laptop with webcam, microphone, stable internet (minimum 2 Mbps), updated browser, and basic computer skills.
