## Academic Rules & Regulations

### Credit System

- **Total Credits:** Varies by course (typically 4-6 credits per course)
- **Credit Distribution:** Combination of theory, practical, and project credits

### Attendance Requirements

**Compulsory Activities:**
- All prescribed academic activities listed in course outlines are mandatory
- Regular participation in online sessions and activities expected
- Attendance tracked through LMS participation

**Exemption Policy:**
- Exemptions only granted for:
  - Accident (with supporting documentation)
  - Family calamity (genuine emergencies)

### Program Evaluation & Grading

**Continuous Internal Assessment (30%):**
- Conducted by course faculty throughout the semester
- Includes: assignments, quizzes, presentations, class participation
- Minimum passing requirement for internal assessment: 40%

**University Examination (70%):**
- Conducted by the university at end of each semester
- Examination modes:
  - Written paper (theory)
  - Practical test (lab-based)
  - Oral test/viva
  - Presentation
  - Combination of above methods

**Minimum Passing Criteria:**
- Must pass both internal assessment (≥40%) and university examination (≥36%)
- Overall minimum: 36% marks in each course
- Grade other than 'FF' required to earn credits

**Grading System:**
- **SGPA:** Semester Grade Point Average
- **CGPA:** Cumulative Grade Point Average across all semesters
- **Grade Points:** Range from 0 to 10
- **Letter Grades:** O, A+, A, B+, B, C, P, F, FF

**SGPA Calculation:**
```
SGPA = (Sum of (Credit × Grade Point) for all courses in semester) / (Total credits in semester)
```

**CGPA Calculation:**
```
CGPA = (Sum of (Credit × Grade Point) for all courses across all semesters) / (Total credits earned)
```

### Step-by-Step Admission Guide

   - Visit: https://admission.charusat.ac.in/

4. **Application Fee Payment**

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Admission & Eligibility

**Q: Is there an age limit for admission?**

A: Minimum age is 18 years. There is no upper age limit for admission.

**Q: What documents are required for admission?**

A: Required documents include 10th & 12th marksheets, identity proof, address proof, passport photos, and signature.

### Course & Curriculum

**Q: How many semesters are there in Online BCA?**

A: The program has 6 semesters (3 years) with maximum duration of 10 semesters (5 years).

**Q: Are practical sessions included in online mode?**

A: Yes, virtual labs and practical demonstrations are provided through the LMS.

### Examination & Assessment

**Q: How are examinations conducted?**

A: Examinations are conducted online with proctoring. You can take exams from home with a stable internet connection.

**Q: What is the passing criteria?**

A: Minimum 36% marks in each course overall, with at least 40% in internal assessment and passing the university examination.

### Degree & Career

**Q: Is the Online BCA degree recognized?**

A: Yes, the degree is UGC-entitled and equivalent to regular campus-based BCA degree.

**Q: What are career prospects after BCA?**

A: Career opportunities include software development, web design, database administration, system analysis, IT support, and more.

### Technical & LMS

**Q: What device and internet speed is required?**

A: You need a computer/laptop with webcam, microphone, and stable internet connection (minimum 2 Mbps).

**Q: How do I access study materials?**

A: Study materials are available 24/7 through the Learning Management System (LMS) portal.

**Q: What if I face technical issues during exams?**

A: Technical support is available during examination hours. Contact the exam helpline immediately.
