# Refund Policy (as per University Guidelines)

Refund mode: Cheque or NEFT (from the Account Section of CHARUSAT).  
Cancellation form: https://charusat.online/catalogs/Adm_Can_Frm.docx

## Refund slabs

| Sr. No. | Timing of request | Refund amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Request received at the University Office before the closure of admission to the concerned programme or the date of commencement of classes, whichever is later. | Fee refund after deduction of Rs. 1500/- as administrative charges. |
| 2 | Request received at the University Office after the commencement of the academic session. | Fee deduction on pro-rata basis from the date of commencement of classes plus Rs. 1500/- as processing charges. |
| 3 | Request received at the University Office after the date of the first mid-term review. | No refund (except for the security deposit). |

## Notes

1. In case of an admission cancellation request of the student after the deadline mentioned in the table, the student shall be liable to pay the fees for the current semester.
2. All new admission cancellation applications shall be processed once the academic session starts.

## When each slab applies (plain description)

- **Slab 1:** Applies if the cancellation request reaches the University Office **before** admission closure for the concerned programme or before/on the **commencement of classes** — whichever is later. After deduction of **Rs. 1500/-** as administrative charges, the remaining fee is refunded.
- **Slab 2:** Applies if the cancellation request reaches the University Office **after commencement of the academic session**. Refund is calculated on a **pro-rata** basis from the date of commencement of classes, plus **Rs. 1500/-** as processing charges are deducted.
- **Slab 3:** Applies if the cancellation request reaches the University Office **after the date of the first mid-term review**. **No refund** is made except for the **security deposit**.
