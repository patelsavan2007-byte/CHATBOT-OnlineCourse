# Online BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration) - Complete Guide

## Overview

The Online Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) programme at CHARUSAT is meticulously designed to empower learners with essential business knowledge, skills, and competencies for success in the evolving field of business administration. It offers specialized expertise in Finance Management, Human Resource Management, and Marketing Management.

### Program Details

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Duration** | 3 years (6 semesters) |
| **Total Credits** | 133 |
| **Annual Fee** | ₹50,000 INR |
| **Intake (2024-25)** | 1,000 students |
| **Mode** | 100% Online |
| **Delivery** | Asynchronous with live sessions |
| **Specializations** | Finance, HR, Marketing |
| **Medium of Instruction** | English |

## Curriculum & Course Structure

### Semester System

- **Total Semesters:** 6 (3 years)
- **Semester Duration:** Approximately 4-5 months each
- **Academic Year:** Two semesters per year
- **Credit System:** Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)

### Semester-wise Curriculum

#### **Semester I (Foundation Semester)**
- Communicative English
- Principles and Practices of Management
- Logical Analysis and Decision Making
- Computer Fundamentals and Applications
- Skills for Management Professionals
- Environmental Science

**Focus:** Building foundational business concepts and communication skills

#### **Semester II (Core Business Concepts)**
- Fundamentals of Economics
- Quantitative Techniques
- Basics of Accounting
- Organizational Behavior
- Business Case Analysis

**Focus:** Quantitative skills and basic business operations

#### **Semester III (Functional Management)**
- Financial Management
- Human Resource Management
- International Business
- IT in Business Management
- Marketing Management

**Focus:** Specialized functional areas of business

#### **Semester IV (Advanced Concepts)**
- Human Values and Professional Ethics
- Fundamentals of Research
- Project Management and Operations Research
- Entrepreneurship Development and Innovation Management
- Business Taxation
- Seminar on Contemporary Issues in Management

#### **Semester V (Specialization & Practical)**
- Organizational Attachment Programme (OAP)

#### **Semester VI (Capstone & Specialization)**

## Admission Process

### Step-by-Step Application Guide

**Step 1: Online Registration**
- Visit: https://admission.charusat.ac.in/
- Click "New Registration" or "Apply Now"

**Step 2: Program Selection**
- Select "BBA – Online" from program list
- Choose specialization preference (Finance/HR/Marketing)
- Select academic year

**Step 3: Personal Information**
- Full name (as per Class 10 certificate)
- Date of birth, gender, contact details
- Permanent and correspondence address
- Parent/guardian details

**Step 4: Academic Background**
- Any other qualifications

**Step 5: Document Upload**
- Upload signature
- Upload Class 10 and 12 marksheets
- Upload identity and address proof
- Upload any additional certificates

**Step 6: Application Fee Payment**
- Pay application fee through online gateway
- Keep payment receipt for reference

**Step 7: Form Review & Submission**
- Review all entered information
- Verify document uploads
- Accept Terms & Conditions
- Submit application

**Step 8: Admission Review**
- Admissions team reviews your application
- Documents are verified
- Eligibility criteria are checked
- You may be contacted for clarifications via email/SMS/phone

**Step 9: Admission Decision**
- Receive admission status via email
- Provisional admission letter issued
- Instructions for fee payment

**Step 10: Enrollment & Fee Payment**
- Complete enrollment formalities
- Pay first-semester fees
- Receive LMS login credentials
- Receive Student ID

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### About BBA Programme

**Q: What is the duration of the Online BBA?**

A: The programme duration is 3 years (6 semesters) with a maximum completion time of 5 years to accommodate working professionals.

**Q: Are there any offline requirements?**

A: No, the programme is 100% online. All classes, exams, and interactions happen through the Learning Management System.

- **HR:** For human resources, talent management, organizational development
- **Marketing:** For sales, branding, digital marketing, consumer relations

### Academic Questions

**Q: How are exams conducted?**

A: Exams are conducted online with proctoring. You need a computer with webcam, microphone, and stable internet connection.
