## 1. General Questions

### Q01: What online programs are offered?
- **Answer:** CHARUSAT offers four UGC-entitled online degree programmes: Bachelor of Business Administration (Online BBA), Bachelor of Computer Applications (Online BCA), Master of Business Administration (Online MBA), and Master of Computer Applications (Online MCA).
- **Citation:** ackend/knowledge_base/home.md (Sections: Programs Offered); ackend/knowledge_base/mandatory-disclosures.md.

### Q02: Is the online degree recognized by UGC?
- **Answer:** Yes, the online degree programmes are recognized and entitled by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and Distance Education Bureau (DEB). CHARUSAT is a NAAC A+ accredited university.
- **Citation:** ackend/knowledge_base/mandatory-disclosures.md; ackend/knowledge_base/about.md.

### Q03: Is the degree valid for government and private jobs?
- **Answer:** Not in the documents -> contact Admissions: Mr. Darshit Patel (7434055553) / Mr. Kirti Patel (7600257365). *(Note: UGC regulations confer equivalence to conventional degrees, but direct job eligibility statements are not in KB files).*

### Q04: Is the online degree equivalent to a regular degree?
- **Answer:** Yes, as per UGC regulations for online education, degrees acquired through open and distance learning (ODL) or online mode from recognized universities are treated as equivalent to degrees acquired through conventional mode.
- **Citation:** ackend/knowledge_base/mandatory-disclosures.md; ackend/knowledge_base/terms-conditions.md.

### Q05: Who is eligible for this program?
- **Answer:** 
  - **Online BBA:** 10+2 (Higher Secondary) in any stream from a recognized board with minimum 45% marks (40% for reserved category).
  - **Online BCA:** 10+2 with English and Mathematics / Statistics / Business Maths / Computer / Accountancy with minimum 45% marks (40% for reserved category).
  - **Online MBA:** Bachelor\'s degree of minimum 3 years from a recognized university with minimum 50% marks (45% for reserved category).
  - **Online MCA:** BCA / Bachelor\'s degree in Computer Science or equivalent, OR any Bachelor\'s degree with Mathematics at 10+2 or Graduation level, with minimum 50% marks (45% for reserved category).
- **Citation:** ackend/knowledge_base/programs/online_bba.md (Eligibility Criteria); ackend/knowledge_base/programs/online_bca.md; ackend/knowledge_base/programs/online_mba.md; ackend/knowledge_base/programs/online_mca.md; PPR PDFs.

### Q31: What is the refund policy if I cancel my admission?
- **Answer:** Governed by UGC Fee Refund Policy (Fees Refund Policy.pdf):
  - 100% refund (less processing fee max ₹1,000): 15 days or more before the formally notified last date of admission.
  - 90% refund: Less than 15 days before the formally notified last date of admission.
  - 80% refund: 15 days or less after the formally notified last date of admission.
  - 50% refund: 30 days or less, but more than 15 days, after formally notified last date of admission.
  - 0% refund: More than 30 days after formally notified last date of admission.
- **Citation:** ackend/knowledge_base/pdfs/Fees Refund Policy.pdf (Section 4: Refund Percentage Matrix).

### Q42: How much weightage do assignments carry?
- **Answer:** Continuous internal evaluation weightage is:

  - Online BCA: Document states 50% internal / 50% external in text, and 30% internal / 70% external in course tables.
  - Online MBA: 50% internal / 50% external end-semester exam.
  - Online MCA: 30% internal / 70% external end-semester exam.
- **Citation:** ackend/knowledge_base/pdfs/PPR_Online BBA.pdf; PPR_Online BCA.pdf; PPR_Online MBA.pdf; PPR_Online MCA.pdf.

### Q43: What is the passing criteria?
- **Answer:** Students must score a minimum of 36% marks in continuous internal assessment, 36% marks in the end-semester examination, and 36% marks in aggregate for each course/subject to pass.
- **Citation:** ackend/knowledge_base/pdfs/PPR_Online BBA.pdf (Grading & Passing Scheme); PPR_Online BCA.pdf; PPR_Online MBA.pdf; PPR_Online MCA.pdf.

### Q44: Can I appear for backlogs online?
- **Answer:** Supplementary and backlog examinations are conducted through the online examination system as per university exam rules -> contact Examinations: Mr. Snehal Patel (9409572216) / Mr. Sanket Patel (BCA/MCA) (8460549025).
- **Citation:** ackend/knowledge_base/terms-conditions.md; ackend/knowledge_base/programs/contact.md.

### Q45: How is the grading system structured?
- **Answer:** 10-point letter grading scale:
  - 90–100%: Grade O (Outstanding, 10.0)
  - 80–89%: Grade A+ (Excellent, 9.0)
  - 70–79%: Grade A (Very Good, 8.0)
  - 60–69%: Grade B+ (Good, 7.0)
  - 50–59%: Grade B (Above Average, 6.0)
  - 40–49%: Grade C (Average, 5.0)
  - 36–39%: Grade P (Pass, 4.0)
  - Below 36%: Grade F (Fail, 0.0)
- **Citation:** All four PPR PDFs (PPR_Online BBA.pdf, PPR_Online BCA.pdf, PPR_Online MBA.pdf, PPR_Online MCA.pdf).
