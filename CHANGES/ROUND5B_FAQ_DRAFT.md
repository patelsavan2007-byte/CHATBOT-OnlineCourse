## Programme Overview & Eligibility

### Q1: What online programmes does CHARUSAT offer?
- **Official Answer:** CHARUSAT offers four UGC-entitled online degree programmes: Online Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA), Online Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA), Online Master of Business Administration (MBA), and Online Master of Computer Applications (MCA).
- **Exact Citation:** `online_bba.md`, Line 6; `online_bca.md`, Line 6; `online_mba.md`, Line 6; `online_mca.md`, Line 6.

### Q2: What is the eligibility criteria for Online BCA?
- **Official Answer:** 10+2 (Higher Secondary) examination passed in any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts) or its equivalent from a recognized board.
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online BCA.pdf`, Page 3, Section 2 ("Eligibility Criteria").

### Q3: What is the eligibility criteria for Online BBA?
- **Official Answer:** 10+2 (Higher Secondary) examination passed in any stream or its equivalent from a recognized board.
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online BBA.pdf`, Page 3, Section 2.

### Q4: What is the eligibility criteria for Online MBA?
- **Official Answer:** Bachelor's degree of minimum 3 years duration in any discipline from a recognized University with at least 50% marks (45% for reserved category candidates).
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online MBA.pdf`, Page 3, Section 2.

### Q5: What is the eligibility criteria for Online MCA?
- **Official Answer:** Passed BCA / Bachelor Degree in Computer Science Engineering or equivalent degree; OR Passed B.Sc. / B.Com. / B.A. with Mathematics at 10+2 level or graduation level (with additional bridge courses as per university norms).
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online MCA.pdf`, Page 3, Section 2.
- *Note on Contradiction:* The marketing FAQ on `online_mca.md` line 14 incorrectly lists "10+2 in any stream" due to a template copy error; the PPR official eligibility requires a Bachelor's degree.

## Fees, Installments & Refund Policies

### Q6: What is the total fee for Online BBA?
- **Official Answer:** Total tuition fee is Rs. 1,50,000 (Rs. 25,000 per semester across 6 semesters) plus examination fee of Rs. 1,500 per semester (Rs. 9,000 total exam fee), bringing the overall fee to Rs. 1,59,000.
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online BBA.pdf`, Page 10 ("Fee Structure").

### Q7: What is the total fee for Online BCA?
- **Official Answer:** Total tuition fee is Rs. 1,50,000 (Rs. 25,000 per semester across 6 semesters).
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online BCA.pdf`, Page 10 ("Fee Structure").

### Q8: What is the total fee for Online MBA?
- **Official Answer:** Total tuition fee is Rs. 1,60,000 (Rs. 40,000 per semester across 4 semesters).
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online MBA.pdf`, Page 11 ("Fee Structure").

### Q9: What is the total fee for Online MCA?
- **Official Answer:** Total tuition fee is Rs. 1,40,000 (Rs. 35,000 per semester across 4 semesters).
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online MCA.pdf`, Page 10 ("Fee Structure").

### Q10: Are there hidden charges or examination fees?
- **Official Answer:** Examination fees are Rs. 1,500 per semester (for BBA/BCA/MBA/MCA as specified in PPR evaluation guidelines). There are no other hidden tuition surcharges.
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online BBA.pdf`, Page 10; `PPR_Online BCA.pdf`, Page 10.

### Q11: What is the refund policy for fee cancellation?
- **Official Answer:** Refunds are processed in accordance with UGC refund guidelines based on the number of days prior to the formally notified last date of admission.
- **Exact Citation:** `terms-conditions.md`, Line 32.

### Q12: Is EMI or installment facility available?
- **Official Answer:** Yes, flexible installment and no-cost EMI options are available via university banking partners.
- **Exact Citation:** `terms-conditions.md`, Line 42.

## Curriculum, Internships & Evaluation

### Q13: Does Online BBA include an internship or project?
- **Official Answer:** Yes. In Semester VI, students undertake the "Organizational Attachment Programme" (Course Code BB606, 6 Credits).
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online BBA.pdf`, Page 8, Line 76.

### Q14: Does Online MBA include an internship or industry project?
- **Official Answer:** Yes. In Semester IV, students undertake the "Comprehensive Project - Industrial Training" (Course Code MB405, 6 Credits).
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online MBA.pdf`, Page 7, Line 71.

### Q15: Does Online MCA include an internship or dissertation?
- **Official Answer:** Yes. In Semester IV, students undertake the "Dissertation / Project Work - Industrial Training" (Course Code MC406, 12 Credits).
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online MCA.pdf`, Page 6, Line 73.

### Q16: What is the evaluation weightage (Internal vs External)?
- **Official Answer:**
  - Online BCA: 50% Internal (Continuous Evaluation) and 50% Final University Examination (`PPR_Online BCA.pdf`, p. 10); note that course structure table on page 6 shows 30% Internal / 70% University Exam.
  - Online MBA: 50% Internal / 50% University Exam (`PPR_Online MBA.pdf`, p. 6).
  - Online BBA: 30% Internal / 70% University Exam (`PPR_Online BBA.pdf`, p. 8).
  - Online MCA: 30% Internal / 70% University Exam (`PPR_Online MCA.pdf`, pp. 4-6).
- **Exact Citation:** `PPR_Online BCA.pdf`, pp. 6 & 10; `PPR_Online MBA.pdf`, p. 6; `PPR_Online BBA.pdf`, p. 8; `PPR_Online MCA.pdf`, pp. 4-6.
