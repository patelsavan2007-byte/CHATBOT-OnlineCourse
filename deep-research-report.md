# CHARUSAT Online Education Programs

CHARUSAT (Charotar University of Science & Technology) is a UGC-approved university in Anand, Gujarat, with NAAC A+ accreditation.  In recent years it has heavily promoted online learning: for 2022–25 it reported **19,696+ NPTEL enrollments** and **85k+ Coursera enrollments** among students and faculty.  CHARUSAT even signed an MoU with Coursera to offer premium industry-relevant courses, under which **113 programs** were launched and have driven 85,000+ enrollments with 61,000+ completions.  These metrics – along with 64 NPTEL Gold Medals (33 earned in 2025 alone) and 65 NPTEL Star Awardees – “position CHARUSAT among the leading institutions in India’s online education ecosystem”.

## Degree Programs Offered Online

CHARUSAT’s dedicated online institutes offer traditional degree programs entirely via the web.  In particular, **CMPICA** (Smt. Chandaben Mohanbhai Patel Institute of Computer Applications) offers the online BCA and MCA degrees, while **I2IM** (Indukaka Ipcowala Institute of Management) offers the online BBA and MBA degrees.  All programs follow UGC guidelines and are recognized by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). Each program is delivered fully online through a learning management system (LMS), with mandatory online proctored exams.

- **Accreditation:** CHARUSAT is NAAC A+ and UGC-approved.  All online degrees are valid for international evaluations (e.g. WES). 
- **Delivery Mode:**  100% online via LMS.  Students access video lectures, e-books, quizzes and assignments asynchronously, plus occasional live webinars.  Exams are conducted online under proctored conditions.
- **Admission:**  Applications are submitted online via the admissions portal.  Entry criteria are standard (see below), and admissions committees review all documents before confirming enrollment.

Below are summaries of each online program (details sourced from CHARUSAT’s official site):

### Online BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)

- **Overview:** A **3-year (six-semester)** program designed “to empower learners with knowledge, skills, and competencies necessary for success in … business administration”.  It offers specializations in Finance, Human Resource, and Marketing management.
- **Credits & Fee:**  Total **133 credits**; annual fee ₹50,000.
- **Eligibility:**  Completion of 10+2 (secondary) education or equivalent.  A minimum score/CGPA is set by the admissions committee, and candidates must have adequate English proficiency.
- **Curriculum (highlights):**  Year-wise subjects include Management Principles, Economics, Accounting, Organizational Behavior, Marketing, etc. (For example, Semester I includes Communicative English, Principles of Management, Computer Fundamentals, etc..)  The program culminates in project work and specialization electives in the final year.
- **Outcomes:** Graduates learn to analyze contemporary management issues, communicate effectively, understand global perspectives, and engage in lifelong learning.
- **Delivery:** Entirely online via LMS.  CHARUSAT notes that “teaching and learning happen using a Learning Management System (LMS), which can be accessed by every enrolled student”.

### Online BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)

- **Overview:** A **3-year** program focused on computer science and applications, aligned with CMPICA’s mission of fostering creativity and innovation.  Emphasis is on practical, project-based learning to develop teamwork and communication skills.
- **Credits & Fee:**  Total **145 credits**; annual fee ₹50,000.
- **Eligibility:**  Passed 12th standard (any stream) with English as a subject. (Admissions follow CHARUSAT’s regulations.)
- **Curriculum (highlights):**  Core topics cover programming, web design, database systems, software engineering, mobile app development, etc. (E.g., Semester I includes Programming Fundamentals, Web Designing, etc., while later semesters include Java, Software Testing and a Project Work.)
- **Outcomes:** Graduates will be able to devise computing solutions for real problems, communicate and manage projects, and pursue careers in IT/software domains.
- **Delivery:** Fully online via LMS.  CHARUSAT confirms the online BCA follows UGC regulations with no on-campus requirement; students can study from any internet-connected device.

### Online MBA (Master of Business Administration)

- **Overview:** A **2-year** program aimed at non-traditional learners (working professionals, entrepreneurs, homemakers, etc.) for whom regular MBA is infeasible.  It offers the same rigor in management education with flexible online access.
- **Credits & Fee:**  Total **100 credits**; annual fee ₹75,000.
- **Eligibility:**  Bachelor’s degree (any discipline) with English as a subject.  (Admissions follow CHARUSAT and regulatory guidelines.)
- **Curriculum (highlights):**  Core management courses: Economics, Accounting, Analytics, HR, Marketing, Finance, Strategic Management, etc.  In the second year, students select specialization courses in Finance, HR, or Marketing (e.g. “Financial Modeling”, “Strategic HRM”, “Digital Marketing” etc.), plus a comprehensive industry project.
- **Outcomes:** MBA graduates gain leadership and analytical skills for business; they learn to solve management problems, make data-driven decisions, and understand global economic/legal/ethical issues.
- **Delivery:** Entirely online. CHARUSAT states the MBA “programme is entirely online” via the LMS, with the same support (e-lectures, e-books, forums, live doubt sessions) as on-campus students.

### Online MCA (Master of Computer Applications)

- **Overview:** A **2-year** advanced computing program for non-traditional learners (full-time professionals, remote students, etc.).  It covers software development, databases, networking and emerging fields like cybersecurity.
- **Credits & Fee:**  Total **108 credits**; annual fee ₹75,000.
- **Eligibility:**  Bachelor’s degree (any discipline) with English as a subject, per regulatory norms.
- **Curriculum (highlights):**  Semesters include Cloud Computing, Java EE, .NET Architecture, Python, Agile/DevOps, Mobile Programming, Data Analytics, Software Testing, and a final dissertation/project.
- **Outcomes:** Graduates will master advanced CS techniques: they can design and develop complex software systems, adapt emerging technologies, communicate technical concepts, and lead project teams.
- **Delivery:** Fully online via LMS, following UGC guidelines on online education. Students never need to attend campus — all lectures, labs and exams are handled online.

### Key Takeaways

- **Modern Platforms:** CHARUSAT’s online programs leverage MOOC platforms. For example, the university’s Coursera initiative includes 113 launched specializations and certificates, with student enrollments exceeding **85,000**. Its NPTEL chapter has achieved an AAA institutional rating and dozens of gold medals.
- **Student Support:** Each student is assigned a mentor and has access to discussion forums and live webinars for doubt-clearing.  Course materials (video lectures, e-books, quizzes) are available 24/7 on the LMS.
- **Validity:** The online degrees follow UGC regulations and are recognized for higher education and employment.  CHARUSAT explicitly states that its online degrees are valid for overseas credential evaluation (WES).
- **Campus & Contact:** CHARUSAT’s campus is in Changa (Anand), Gujarat. For admissions queries, students can contact CHARUSAT at +91-7434055556 or email online-admissions@charusat.ac.in (as listed on the programme pages).
